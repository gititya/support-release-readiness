# RR-001: support operating rules

Owner: Support readiness lead Mira. Approved: 2026-10-09 09:00 UTC. Applies to this fictional release only.

## Evidence and authority

Product lead Jo approves customer behavior. Billing lead Sam approves refund and billing guidance. Engineering lead Dev supplies technical tests and fixes. Support readiness lead Mira recommends support readiness and rollout pauses. Release owner Jo authorizes launches and pauses. Engineering on-call Dev executes rollout changes. Vendor manager Lee confirms staffing and vendor access. Training owner Nia confirms training coverage; content owner Ren owns public and internal help; support operations owner Alex owns CRM and routing.

Product documents define approved intended behavior. Test records are evidence of simulated checks, not proof of every possible outcome. A help article or training slide cannot override approved behavior. If a later approved change affects guidance, review that guidance again. Earlier records remain historical evidence. Plans and drafts are not completed actions; an owner name is not an approval.

## Before launch

Product must send the internal announcement at least 72 hours before launch, naming the behavior revision and audience. Mira adapts it into a support notice. Internal and vendor agents need the current notice, training, internal guidance and access before serving exposed customers. Sending and acknowledgement are distinct. Vendor coverage requires a roster and a named lead's acknowledgement, including acknowledgement of late notice changes.

Cancellation and billing guidance must match the approved flow. Public content must be published for the exposed cohort. Every known billing issue needs safe guidance, an escalation route and the product owner's acceptance before exposure. Support launch readiness is not ready if this evidence is missing or contradicts the current behavior.

CRM contact reasons must be available on internal and vendor case forms, with old reasons mapped to new ones. Staffing requires stated volume assumptions, an AHT assumption (average handling time), reserve coverage and vendor acceptance. A planning estimate is not a demand forecast. If no infrastructure changes are needed, an owner can record a scoped no-change assessment rather than inventing work.

## Rollout and follow-up

The first hour is limited to 2,000 exposed accounts. Compare contact rates per 1,000 exposed accounts over equal one-hour windows. Recommend a pause if the rate is at least twice the baseline, or if two separate customers have matching reports of an unexpected charge after confirmed cancellation. These are conservative fictional policy thresholds, not proof of statistical significance or causality.

The agent can recommend and prepare an escalation draft. It cannot authorize or execute a pause. A pause stops new exposure; it does not roll back a deployed fix or repair already affected accounts.

For a defect, record engineering status, deployment evidence, affected customers and their responses separately. Do not mark customer impact resolved solely because a fix deployed or a ticket was marked duplicate. Mira can close the feedback loop after each affected customer's outcome is confirmed, or explicitly document an unresolved customer and follow-up owner.
