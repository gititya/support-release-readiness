# Support content and training package S1

Owners: Ren (content), Nia (training). Updated: 2026-10-11 09:00 UTC. Based on product proposal v1. Status: draft.

## Public article: cancel your subscription

Open Manage Plan and click Cancel subscription. Your cancellation is complete and there will be no further renewals. You keep access to the end of your paid term. Cancelling does not refund that term; a person must review refund requests.

## Public article: reminders

Feature Settings has an optional Renewal reminders toggle. When enabled it emails you seven days before renewal. Changing it affects messages, not the plan or amount.

## Agent training

The cancellation button completes cancellation. Ask the customer to refresh Billing to see the new date. If the date is unchanged, reassure them that another charge will not happen. Refunds require billing review; do not promise one.
