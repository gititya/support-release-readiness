# RR-001 product proposal v1

Owner: Jo. Status: proposal. Updated: 2026-10-09 10:00 UTC.

## Goal and scope

Reduce contacts from customers who think cancelling immediately removes paid access or who cannot tell whether cancellation finished. First cohort: 2,000 self-serve monthly Pro accounts at $49/month. Starter at $9 and Business at $149 remain unchanged; annual and administrator-managed accounts are excluded.

Manage Plan will show a confirmation step, access end date and a success state after billing records cancellation. A planned email will restate the access end date. Refunds still require billing review and are never automatic. Renewal reminders currently run seven days before renewal when the customer's toggle is on.

Target launch: 2026-10-16 10:00 UTC. Reminder changes and cancellation-confirmation wording are under review; this is not approved behavior.
