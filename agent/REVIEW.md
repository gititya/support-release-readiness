# Support preparation through the release

Establish the phase, approved product behavior, affected users and launch time from current records. Distinguish a proposal, an approved requirement, a test result and an observed customer report. State what is missing or conflicting.

At planning, trace customer friction to product changes that could prevent contacts. Keep unanswered behavior and safe-handling decisions actionable, with supplied decision owners and the decision needed. Final training, staffing and publication stay future work unless company planning rules make them due now.

Before launch, cover these areas with current evidence:

| Area | What matters |
| --- | --- |
| planning | Approved behavior, affected cohort, open decisions and changes that prevent contacts |
| training | Current guidance, completed training, roster coverage and agent access |
| help | Correct public/internal instructions, publication and safe handling |
| vendors | Current notices, access, preparation and acknowledgement; company evidence can establish no vendor scope |
| staffing | Contact/handling assumptions, accepted coverage and reserve; a request is not a booking |
| operations | CRM categories/mappings on affected forms, routing and scoped infrastructure assessment |
| communications | Current product announcement under the company's lead rule, adapted support/vendor notice, delivery and acknowledgement |
| known issues | Supported workarounds, safe advice, escalation and required approvals |

For a ready recommendation, supply the helper's coverage entries for all eight areas, each verified or not applicable with a reason and current non-draft records. Findings are unresolved work only. Put resolved risks, healthy preparation and pending human launch authority in observations, not critical/major findings. A pending launch decision is a separate authority state, not a support-readiness defect. The helper rejects a ready recommendation with open serious findings. This does not authorize launch. The helper checks references and record states, not the meaning of a passage; do not cite a product plan as proof that training happened.

When product changes, compare named behavior facts with completed preparation. Reopen affected guidance, training or communications. Keep unchanged CRM, routing, refunds and accepted staffing complete. An announcement that cannot meet its lead rule needs a compliant launch-date change or an explicitly authorized policy exception; sending another draft does not repair the date.

Use critical for false confirmation of a consequential customer action, unsupported billing/refund assurances, missing safe handling or approval for a known billing issue, materially stale agent guidance, or a supplied rollout pause condition. Use major for other unmet preparation and follow-up work. Explain consequence and action. A supplied company severity rule takes precedence; explain the difference. Do not turn every useful extra test into a mandatory launch block.

At rollout, compare equal-length supplied windows and affected-user exposure, contact categories and customer reports. Use the helper's per-1,000 rates, rate ratio, rate increase and contact-count change. A zero baseline has no finite ratio. Matching charge reports warrant billing investigation; they do not establish a settled capture or refund entitlement. Recommend pause when the supplied rule warrants it; record recommendation, human authorization and execution separately. Healthy supplied signals support continued monitoring within the authorized cohort, not wider exposure.

After launch, separate issue identification, engineering fix, deployment, remedy and customer confirmation. A duplicate issue closure is not a second fix. A processed refund is not a customer confirming receipt. Track each supplied named affected case, its owner and the feedback delivered to product/engineering. If the responsible team's record says an unnamed group was individually confirmed and resolved, preserve that supported group outcome; do not invent a mandatory request for separate case IDs or reopen that group without a conflict or company rule. Follow-up complete applies only to the supported cases, not authorization to resume a paused launch.

Completion is supported by evidence and a person's acceptance, not the agent's confidence. Never invent delivery, staffing commitments, approvals, pauses, captures or customer recovery. Identify the next useful owner action when evidence is insufficient.
