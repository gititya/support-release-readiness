## Unchanged decision rule

- **Keep it if:** B finds every critical finding on both runs, achieves at least 90% major-finding recall in each sequence, returns all six expected phase recommendations, passes every required calculation and state distinction, and invents no completion or unsupported block. It must exceed A's major-finding recall by at least 10 percentage points in each sequence. Keep means proceed with a small local pilot, not production approval.
- **Use it only sometimes if:** B passes all safety and phase requirements but does not beat A by that margin, or scores below 90% on major findings. Use a human-reviewed report for the phases it handles correctly; inspect failures before widening use. If A also passes, prefer the simpler workflow until a further test shows value from the added parts.
- **Drop it if:** B invents completion or authority, misses any critical finding on either run, incorrectly clears the late-change snapshot, or misses the launch-day pause recommendation. Stop the proposed version, preserve its evidence and write why. A revised version requires a newly declared experiment, not a softened threshold.

A win here would support a further unseen-release test; it cannot establish broad advantage, enterprise coverage or fewer customer contacts.
